Research

Stochastic ageing classes

Nonparametric tests for exponentiality against ageing alternatives — DFR, UBAC(2), EBUCL and related classes, in univariate and multivariate settings.

U-statistic asymptotics

Test statistics built from U-statistic kernels: null and alternative asymptotic behaviour, consistency, and Pitman efficiency against standard alternatives.

Maintenance and replacement policy

Optimal age-replacement under dependent failure processes, including doubly geometric and Hawkes-type intensity models.

Remaining useful life prediction

Degradation modelling and conformal prediction intervals for RUL, validated on benchmark battery and turbofan datasets.
